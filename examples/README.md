# Example Projects

Sample designs demonstrating the application's capabilities. To open one, start the app and use the **Import** button in the top bar (folder icon) — imported files always become new projects, so they never overwrite your work.

## starter-cottage.dreamhouse.json

A compact two-room cottage on a 12 × 9 m plot. Demonstrates:

- Plot with per-side setbacks and a south-facing road
- Perimeter and partition walls; the gable-end walls are 5 m tall and are automatically trimmed to the roof slope (pitched gable walls)
- A 30° gable roof with an overhang, one skylight, and a dormer
- Doors, sliding and casement windows with mullions
- Wood and tile room finishes, production furniture models, a text annotation
- A composed front elevation (canopy, shuttered window, planter)

## courtyard-villa.dreamhouse.json

A two-story villa on an irregular four-sided plot. Demonstrates:

- Irregular plot geometry with a north angle, and a **customized buildable footprint** with an accepted setback waiver
- Two floors: an open living/kitchen/study ground floor and a master-suite upper floor
- Composed roofs: a hip roof over the bedroom wing plus a **flat terrace roof with parapets** over the study wing
- Double and sliding doors, mullioned windows across styles
- Travertine, tile, and dark-wood floor finishes; a furnished interior including kitchen, study, and a hung chandelier
- A richer elevation: stone cladding layered behind openings, balcony, pergola, downpipe, name board, and lighting

Open the 3D view after importing to see roof composition and wall trimming; select the plot to inspect the setback and footprint tooling.
